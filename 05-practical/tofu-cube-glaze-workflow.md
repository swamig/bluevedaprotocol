# Day-Of Glazing Master-Workflow (Tofu & Veggies)

This is the consolidated day-of workflow to prepare either **Tofu** or **Frozen Veggies** using any of the three core freezer glaze concentrates: [Soy-Sesame](./sesame-sauce-cubes.md), [Coconut-Lemongrass](./coconut-lemongrass-cubes.md), or [Pad Thai Peanut](./pad-thai-peanut-cubes.md). 

---

## 1. Ingredient & Preparation Prep (Day-Of)

Before turning on the heat, prepare your protein or vegetable anchor:

### Tofu Preparation:
1.  **Thaw:** Ensure your extra-firm tofu slab is thawed (fridge overnight or 30-min cold water submerge).
2.  **Boil/Salt Brine:** Bring water to a boil (typically 4 cups, or scale down to 1 cup). Add **1 tsp sea salt** per 4 cups of water (or **¼ tsp salt** per 1 cup of water), along with optional Tamari (1 tbsp per 4 cups / ¾ tsp per 1 cup) and ginger. Boil the sliced 1-inch tofu slabs for **3 to 5 minutes** to open the pores and season the interior.
3.  **Press:** Remove slabs and press between clean towels under a heavy pan for **5 minutes** to dry the surface.
4.  **Crisp:** Pan-sear slabs in 2 tbsp avocado oil for **60–90 seconds per side** (or air-fry at **400°F/204°C for 15–20 minutes**) until golden-crisp. Set aside.

### Frozen Veggies Preparation:
1.  **No Thawing Required:** Use your frozen veggies (broccoli, snap peas, cabbage, green beans) directly from the freezer.
2.  **Flash Stir-Fry:** Heat **1 tbsp avocado oil** in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the frozen veggies and stir-fry for **3 to 5 minutes** until their water evaporates, the raw ice bite is gone, and the edges are slightly seared and tender. Set aside.

## 2. Glaze & Finish Matrix

The three glaze concentrates are melted the same way (on **Low heat** with **2 tbsp water per 2 cubes**), but they require different off-heat finishing anchors to lock in their specific bio-signals:

| Glaze | Pan Melter | Off-Heat Finishing Oil | Garnish & Seeds | Herb Finisher |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Soy-Sesame** | 2 Sesame cubes + 2 tbsp water | **1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil** | **1 tbsp Toasted Sesame Seeds** (dry-toasted 3-5 min until golden) | 5–6 fresh Mint leaves (torn) and/or Cilantro |
| **Coconut-Lemongrass** | 2 Coconut cubes + 2 tbsp water | *None (rely on coconut fat)* + squeeze of fresh **Lime wedge** | **1 tbsp Hemp Hearts** or toasted sesame seeds | Fresh Thai Basil or Cilantro |
| **Pad Thai Peanut** | 2 Peanut cubes + 2–3 tbsp water | *None* + squeeze of fresh **Lime wedge** | **1 tbsp Crushed Roasted Peanuts** (no oil) | Fresh Cilantro + small handful of raw Bean Sprouts |

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Cooking Sequence (~15–20 Min)

1.  **Allicin Activation (00:00):** Peel and crush/mince 2–3 fresh garlic cloves. **Let rest on the cutting board for 10 minutes** to form active allicin (or pull 1 [Rested Garlic Cube](./freeze-system.md) from the freezer to partial-thaw).
2.  **Cook the Anchor (00:02):** Prepare and crisp your Tofu (Boil $\rightarrow$ Press $\rightarrow$ Sear/Air-Fry) or Flash Stir-Fry your Frozen Veggies. Set them aside on a plate.
3.  **Melt the Glaze (00:15):** Turn the pan heat down to **Low**. Drop **2 frozen sauce cubes** of choice and **2 tablespoons of water** directly into the warm pan. Stir gently for 60–90 seconds until the cubes melt and bubble into a thick, glossy glaze coating the pan.
4.  **The Off-Heat Lock (00:17):** **TURN OFF THE HEAT.** 
    *   Stir your rested garlic/garlic cube into the warm glaze. Let it infuse for 30 seconds.
    *   Toss your crispy tofu or seared veggies directly back into the pan. Stir gently to coat them completely in the sticky glaze.
5.  **Finish & Garnish (00:18):** Add the designated finishing oil/lime squeeze, sprinkle with the designated garnish seeds/nuts, and fold in the fresh herbs. 
6.  **Serve (00:20):** Plate immediately and consume within 5 minutes for peak volatile aromatic bioavailability.

---

## 4. Critical Rules
*   **Allicin & Probiotics are Heat-Sensitive:** Never boil your garlic or miso-loaded sauce cubes. The pan **must be off-heat** when adding rested garlic and when tossing the final ingredients to preserve therapeutic enzymes.
*   **Finishing Oils Never See Fire:** Toasted sesame oil must never be heated in the pan; drizzle only at the very end off-heat to prevent smoking off the volatile aromatics.
*   **Seeds/Nuts Go on Dry & Fresh:** Never freeze sesame seeds or peanuts inside the cubes; they turn soggy and lose their active nutrient profiles. Toast and apply them fresh right at plating.
