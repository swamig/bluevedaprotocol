# Kadhi Reference — Marwari · Punjabi · Gujarati Variants (Make-Fresh Doctrine)

Kadhi is a slow-simmered yogurt-besan (gram flour) soup with a tempered spice tadka. It's beloved across North + West India and exists in three major regional styles: **Punjabi** (with pakoras, mild), **Marwari** (no pakoras, slightly sweet with jaggery, leaner), **Gujarati** (sweeter, more tang, lots of curry leaves).

**Why kadhi doesn't cube:** The yogurt-besan slurry must be whisked smooth and simmered carefully (slow, constant stirring) to prevent the yogurt from curdling at heat. The simmer also develops the besan's nutty flavor over ~20 min — there's no shortcut. **However**, all the support aromatics CAN come from existing cubes, dropping active prep time from ~45 min to ~20 min. Cross-link [Freeze System "Pre-Ferment, Then Freeze"](./freeze-system.md) — the same principle applies (post-biotic delivery for kadhi is fresh + live · the yogurt brings the live cultures every time).

## 2. Base Slurry — All Variants Start Here

Common to all three styles · varies in tadka + jaggery.

| Ingredient | Amount (4 servings) |
|---|---|
| Plain whole-milk yogurt (preferably day-old / slightly sour) | 1.5 cups |
| Besan (chickpea flour) | ½ cup |
| Water | 3 cups |
| Turmeric powder | ½ tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Ginger paste (1 cube melted OR 1 tbsp fresh grated) | 1 tbsp |
| Green chili, slit | 1–2 |

**Slurry method:**
1. In a deep pot · whisk yogurt + besan + water + turmeric + salt + ginger + green chili until completely smooth (**NO LUMPS** — lumps don't fully dissolve later)
2. Place on medium · whisk constantly until it begins to steam (~5 min)
3. As soon as it bubbles · drop heat to low · simmer 15–18 min · stirring every 60–90 sec · until thickened and besan's raw smell is gone

> **Yogurt-curdling watchout:** keep stirring · don't let it sit · don't add anything acidic (lemon, tomato) during the simmer — only AFTER off-heat.

## 3. Marwari / Rajasthani Kadhi

The lean style. No pakoras. Slightly sweet from jaggery. Bright with mustard seed tadka.

**Add to slurry method · finish + tadka:**

| Layer | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Jaggery (grated) | 1–2 tsp | Whisk into hot kadhi at end · just before tadka |
| Lemon juice (optional) | 1 tsp | Off-heat at very end · brightens |

**Tadka (Marwari):**
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- 8–10 curry leaves (or 1 curry leaf tadka cube)
- 1 dried red chili (broken)
- ¼ tsp asafoetida (hing)

**Method:** heat ghee · add mustard seeds · when they pop · add cumin · then curry leaves + dried chili + hing · sizzle 5 sec · pour over the kadhi · serve immediately.

**Serve with:** steamed rice · gatte (besan dumplings) · pickled mango · ghee on top.

## 4. Punjabi Kadhi (with Pakoras)

The richer style. Has besan pakoras (fritters) soaking in the kadhi. Mild compared to Marwari.

**Pakora ingredients (makes ~12 small pakoras):**

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Besan | 1 cup |
| Water | ⅓–½ cup (adjust to thick batter) |
| Onion (small, finely chopped) | 1 |
| Spinach (finely chopped, optional) | ½ cup |
| Cumin seeds | ½ tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Avocado oil (for frying) | 2 cups (deep-frying) |

**Pakora method:**
1. Whisk besan + water + cumin + salt to thick smooth batter
2. Fold in onion (and spinach if using)
3. Heat oil to 350°F · drop 1-tbsp dollops · fry 3–4 min until golden · drain
4. Pakoras can be made up to 6 hr ahead · warm before adding to kadhi

**Add to slurry method · finish + tadka:**

| Layer | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Pakoras | 8–12 | Drop into hot kadhi just before serving · let soak 5 min |
| Crushed kasuri methi | 1 tsp | Rub between palms · sprinkle off-heat |

**Tadka (Punjabi):**
- 1 tbsp ghee
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- 2 cloves garlic · sliced thin (OR 1 rested garlic cube · OFF heat after tadka)
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder (added at end · off-heat · for color)
- Optional: 4–6 curry leaves

**Method:** heat ghee · cumin · garlic (sizzle until golden) · off heat · stir in Kashmiri chili (don't burn) · pour over kadhi.

**Serve with:** basmati rice · pickle · kachumber salad.

## 5. Gujarati Kadhi

The sweetest + tangiest style. Lots of curry leaves. Slight ginger-forward.

**Add to slurry method · finish + tadka:**

| Layer | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Jaggery (grated) | 1 tbsp (more than Marwari) | Whisk into hot kadhi · gives the signature sweet-tang balance |
| Extra ginger paste | 1 tsp additional (so 2 tbsp total) | At start with slurry · Gujarati kadhi is ginger-forward |
| Lemon juice OR amchur | 1–2 tsp | Off-heat at end · pronounced tang |

**Tadka (Gujarati):**
- 1 tbsp ghee
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- ⅛ tsp fenugreek seeds (methi dana)
- 12–15 curry leaves (or 1–2 curry leaf tadka cubes — more than other variants)
- 1–2 dried red chilies (broken)
- 1 pinch asafoetida
- ¼ tsp cinnamon (Gujarati-specific signature)
- 2–3 cloves (whole)

**Method:** heat ghee · mustard pop · cumin · methi seeds (brown quickly) · curry leaves + chilies + hing + cinnamon + cloves · sizzle 5 sec · pour over kadhi.

**Serve with:** khichdi · rice · papad · methi thepla ([Sheet 33](./protein-methi-thepla.md)).

## 6. Method timing (~20 min day-of)

| Clock | Action |
|---|---|
| 00:00 | Whisk slurry · pull tadka cube + ginger cube from freezer (5 min thaw) |
| 00:03 | Start heating slurry on medium · whisk constantly |
| 00:08 | Bubbles appear · drop to low · simmer 15 min · stir every 60–90 sec |
| 00:15 | Make tadka in a small pan (parallel) |
| 00:20 | Add finishing layer (jaggery / lemon / kasuri methi per variant) · pour tadka · serve |

---

## 7. Critical Rules

- **Whisk yogurt + besan + water until COMPLETELY smooth** before heat · lumps don't dissolve later
- **Constant stirring on medium-to-low** · stopping = curdling
- **No acid during the simmer.** Lemon · tomato · tamarind all go in OFF heat at the very end · adding mid-simmer cracks the yogurt
- **No bhuna masala cube** — its tomato-onion profile fights kadhi's yogurt tang
- **Day-old yogurt is better.** Slightly sour yogurt yields the canonical sour-tang profile · fresh sweet yogurt makes a flat kadhi
- **Don't cube the kadhi itself.** Yogurt + besan slurry doesn't freeze cleanly · the besan separates and yogurt curdles on thaw. The cube system supports the aromatics, not the base.
- **Make-fresh window:** kadhi is best within 4 hours of cooking · reheats poorly past 24 hr (texture degrades, jaggery sets, oil separates)

## 9. Why kadhi belongs in the protocol

Despite resisting cubing, kadhi delivers:

- **Live yogurt cultures** (every batch · fresh) — daily probiotic anchor that the cube system intentionally doesn't replace
- **Besan protein + fiber** — ~12g protein per serving from the chickpea flour
- **Bio-signal stack** — turmeric + ginger + mustard + cumin + curry leaves + chili = standard protocol overlap
- **Comfort + ritual** — a hot soup that delivers macros + micros without the cube convenience · keeps the make-fresh skill in regular rotation
- Fits within the [Pre-Ferment, Then Freeze doctrine](./freeze-system.md) — the live-culture layer lives in the daily refrigerated stack (yogurt) · kadhi is one of its expressions
