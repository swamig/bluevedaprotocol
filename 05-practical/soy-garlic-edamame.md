# Soy-Garlic Edamame

This recipe is a high-protein, high-fiber snack or side dish designed to deliver a concentrated raw **allicin pulse** alongside complete plant-based proteins. The soy-garlic glaze coats the outside of the edamame pods; as you eat them, you draw the glaze across your taste buds, maximizing sensory signaling.

---

## Ingredients (Per Serving)

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes / Protocol Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Frozen Edamame** (in pods) | 2 cups | ~18g complete protein + rich dietary fiber. |
| **Garlic Cloves** | 3–4 cloves | Mince and **rest 10 minutes** to activate allicin. |
| **Low-Sodium Soy / Tamari** | 1.5 tbsp | Savory umami anchor. |
| **Pitted Medjool Date** (or Honey) | ½ date | Blended or mashed; balances salt without refined sugar. |
| **Avocado Oil** | 1 tsp | Base fat carrier. |
| **Chili Flakes OR Sriracha** | ½ tsp (chili) or 1 tsp (Sriracha) | **TRPV1 Activation.** [Homemade Sriracha](./sriracha-homemade.md) adds capsaicin and organic acids (lactic/acetic) to neutralize iron-blocking phytates. |
| **Toasted Sesame Seeds** | 1 tsp | Garnish finish. |

---

## Method

1.  **Allicin Activation (00:00):** Crush or mince the 3–4 garlic cloves. **Let rest on the cutting board for 10 minutes** to form active allicin.
2.  **Boil/Steam Edamame:** Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add the frozen edamame pods and cook for **4 to 5 minutes** until tender-crisp. Drain thoroughly.
3.  **Prepare the Glaze Base:** In a small pan, combine the avocado oil, soy/tamari, and date paste/honey over low heat. Whisk for 60 seconds until the date paste dissolves and the mixture is warm.
4.  **Off-Heat Allicin Lock:** **TURN OFF THE HEAT.**
    *   Stir the rested minced garlic (and optional chili flakes or 1 tsp [Homemade Sriracha](./sriracha-homemade.md)) directly into the warm sauce in the pan. Let it infuse in the residual heat for 30 seconds. (Using sriracha preserves live probiotic cultures by keeping them away from high cooking heat).
5.  **Toss & Coat:** Add the hot, drained edamame pods to the pan. Toss and stir until the pods are completely coated in the sticky soy-garlic glaze.
6.  **Garnish:** Plate the edamame, sprinkle toasted sesame seeds on top, and serve immediately. 

---

## Critical Rules
*   **Off-Heat Garlic Addition:** Never cook the garlic in hot oil. The heat will destroy the alliinase enzyme, blunting the allicin payload. The pan **must be off-heat** when the rested garlic is added.
*   **The Pod-Draw Effect:** Because the glaze is on the outside of the pod, eating edamame by drawing the pods through your mouth delivers a direct, concentrated burst of raw garlic and soy flavor to your tongue, increasing satiety signaling.
*   **Do Not Eat the Pods:** Eat only the tender green beans inside; discard the tough outer pods.
