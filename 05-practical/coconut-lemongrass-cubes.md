# Coconut Lemongrass Curry Cubes

A concentrated, freezer-stable coconut-lemongrass-galangal curry base designed for fast weeknight stir-fries, noodle bowls, and tofu glazing. This recipe is an alternative spice-base variant of the [Creamy Coconut Protein Cubes](./coconut-protein-cubes.md), optimized specifically for clean lemongrass and citrus signaling.

## Lemons vs. Limes: The Acid Lock

*   **Limes (Preferred):** Limes contain a specific ratio of citric and malic acids that provide the authentic, sharp, bright profile required to balance lemongrass and galangal.
*   **Lemons (Acceptable Fallback):** Lemons work perfectly as a functional substitute. Citric acid is the key agent for the **Acid Lock** (dropping pH below 5.0 to prevent browning and preserve color/flavor in the freezer). If using lemons, increase the quantity slightly to match the sharpness of limes.

## Base Recipe (Single Batch · ~16 mini cubes)

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes / Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Full-fat coconut milk** | 1 can (13.5 oz / ~400 mL) | Do not use light; fat protects against ice crystals. |
| **Lemongrass paste** (or fresh minced) | **1.5 tbsp** | Primary aromatic signal. |
| **Fresh Ginger** (or Galangal) paste | 1 tbsp | Warming gingerol / galangin pulse. |
| **Tamari / Soy Sauce** | 2 tbsp | Umami + salt preservation lock. |
| **Lime Juice** (or Lemon juice) | 2 tbsp | Acid Lock (pH balancer). |
| **Turmeric Powder** | 1 tsp | Curcumin/Nrf2 pulse. |
| **Cumin & Coriander Powder** | 1 tsp each | Digestive anchors. |
| **Cardamom & Cinnamon Powder** | ½ tsp & ¼ tsp | Balancing spices. |
| **Sea Salt** | ½ tsp | Flavor stabilizer. |
| **Arrowroot Powder** | 1 tsp (in 2 tbsp water) | Freeze-stable thickener. |

### Recommended In-Cube Bio-Signals:
*   **White Miso (Unpasteurized):** 1 tbsp (added **off-heat** to preserve live LAB probiotics).
*   **Pea Protein OR Hemp Hearts:** 2 tbsp (satiety + protein booster).
*   **Black Garlic Powder:** 1 tsp (heat-stable SAC sulfur).
*   **Black Pepper (coarse-cracked):** ½ tsp (piperine multiplier).

## Vitamix Batch (12x Scale · ~96 cubes)
*Designed as the optimal volume for a smooth Vitamix vortex. It completely liquefies fresh aromatics and emulsifies the coconut oil matrix.*

*   **Full-Fat Coconut Milk:** 8 cans (~108 oz)
*   **Lemongrass Paste:** **9 tbsp** (approx. ½ cup + 1 tbsp)
*   **Fresh Ginger / Galangal Paste:** ½ cup (8 tbsp)
*   **Tamari / Soy Sauce:** 1 cup (16 tbsp)
*   **Lime Juice (or Lemon juice):** 1 cup (16 tbsp)
*   **Turmeric Powder:** 4 tbsp (12 tsp)
*   **Cumin Powder:** 4 tbsp (12 tsp)
*   **Coriander Powder:** 4 tbsp (12 tsp)
*   **Cardamom Powder:** 2 tbsp (6 tsp)
*   **Cinnamon Powder:** 1 tbsp (3 tsp)
*   **Sea Salt:** 2 tbsp (6 tsp)
*   **Arrowroot Powder:** 4 tbsp slurried in ½ cup cold water
*   **White Miso (Off-Heat):** ½ cup (8 tbsp)
*   **Pea Protein or Hemp Hearts:** 1 cup
*   **Black Garlic Powder:** 4 tbsp (12 tsp)
*   **Black Pepper (coarse-cracked):** 2 tbsp (6 tsp)

---

## Method

1.  **Vitamix Blend:** Pour the coconut milk, lemongrass paste, ginger/galangal paste, tamari, lime (or lemon) juice, salt, and all dry spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, cardamom, cinnamon, black garlic, pepper) into the Vitamix. Blend on High for 60 seconds until completely smooth.
2.  **The Simmer:** Pour the blended mixture into a large pot. Simmer on medium-low for 25–35 minutes until the volume reduces by approximately ⅓ (sauce should coat the back of a spoon).
3.  **Thicken:** Whisk in the arrowroot slurry. Stir constantly for 60–90 seconds until glossy. Do not boil hard.
4.  **Off-Heat Emulsion:** Remove the pot from the heat. Whisk in the **White Miso** and **Pea Protein** until fully integrated. Let cool completely.
5.  **Freeze:** Spoon into mini silicone ice cube trays (~1 tbsp per cell). Freeze overnight, then pop the cubes out and transfer to a labeled zip-lock bag.
