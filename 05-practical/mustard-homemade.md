# Homemade Functional Mustard

This is a teaspoon-measured recipe for a raw, functional mustard designed specifically as an **exogenous myrosinase insurance policy** for your [Protocol Broccoli (Microgreens)](./protocol-broccoli.md). 

Commercial mustard often uses high heat during processing, which destroys the delicate myrosinase enzyme. Making it raw from scratch preserves 100% of the enzyme activity, yielding up to **4x more sulforaphane** from your cruciferous greens.

## Ingredients (Yields ~½ Cup · 1 Glass Jar)

| Ingredient | Amount | Role / Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Yellow mustard seeds** | 3 tbsp | Mild flavor base, rich in mucilage for gut lining. |
| **Brown or Black mustard seeds** | 1 tbsp | High-potency TRPA1 activation + maximum myrosinase yield. |
| **Cold water** | 4 tbsp | **Must be cold (<100°F/38°C).** Heat deactivates myrosinase. |
| **Raw Apple Cider Vinegar (ACV)** | 4 tbsp | With the mother; provides the acid stabilizer (keeps pH <4.0). |
| **Turmeric powder** | ½ tsp | NF-κB signaling synergy + natural yellow pigment. |
| **Sea salt** | ½ tsp | Preservation + flavor balance. |
| **Black pepper (fresh ground)** | ¼ tsp | Piperine absorption multiplier for the turmeric. |
| **Ceylon Cinnamon** | ¼ tsp | Optional; balances tartness without refined sugar. |

## Instructions

1. **The Soak (24–48 Hours):** 
   In a small glass jar, combine the whole yellow and brown/black mustard seeds, cold water, and raw ACV. Cover tightly and let it sit at room temperature for 24 to 48 hours. The seeds will swell and absorb most of the liquid.
2. **The Cold Blend:** 
   Pour the soaked seeds and any residual liquid into a small blender cup (or use a mortar and pestle). Blend until you reach your desired consistency (smooth or coarse stone-ground). 
   *   > [!IMPORTANT]
       > **The Temperature Limit:** Do not run the blender for more than 15–20 seconds at a time. The motor heat must not warm the seeds above 110°F (43°C), or the myrosinase enzyme will degrade.
3. **Add the Bio-Spices:** 
   Stir the Turmeric, Sea Salt, Black Pepper, and Cinnamon directly into the blended mustard. Whisk until fully integrated.
4. **The Mellowing Rest (24 Hours):** 
   Freshly ground mustard will taste intensely bitter and hot due to volatile compounds. Cover the jar and let it sit on the counter at room temperature for 24 hours. This mellows the harshness and stabilizes the enzyme matrix.
5. **Storage:** 
   Store in an airtight glass jar in the refrigerator. Lasts up to **6 months** under refrigeration.

## Critical Rules

*   **Never Use Hot Water:** Hot water denatures myrosinase and releases bitter, unpleasant sulfur compounds. Always use cold or chilled water to activate the seed's enzymes.
*   **No Refined Sugars:** Commercial yellow mustard often contains added sugars or corn starch. This recipe relies on raw ACV acidity and a pinch of cinnamon for natural flavor balance.
*   **The Salad Layer Integration:** Use **1 teaspoon** of this mustard daily, whisked into your [Master Dressing](./sunday-prep.md#block-6-dressing-master-batch-5-min) right before pouring it over your raw [Broccoli Microgreens](./protocol-broccoli.md).
