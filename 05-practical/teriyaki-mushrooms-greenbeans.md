# Mushroom & Green Bean Teriyaki with Basil

This recipe is a high-signaling, fiber-rich vegetable dish that pairs perfectly with the protocol's protein anchors (such as seared tofu or protein patties). It features a unique combination of mushrooms (for immune-modulating beta-glucans and deep umami) and green beans, finished with an aromatic, TRP-active layer of fresh Thai basil.

---

## Ingredients (Per Serving)

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes / Protocol Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Mushrooms** (Shiitake or Cremini) | 8 oz | Beta-glucans (immune support) + natural glutamates. |
| **Fresh Green Beans** | 12 oz | Fiber anchor + chlorophyll layer. |
| **Garlic Cloves** | 4–6 cloves | Mince and **rest 10 minutes** to activate allicin. |
| **Fresh Ginger** | 1 tbsp (grated) | Pro-kinetic gut-motility promoter. |
| **Avocado Oil** | 2 tbsp | High smoke-point, anti-inflammatory monounsaturated fat. |
| **Teriyaki Glaze** | 2–3 tbsp | Or **2 frozen [Soy-Sesame Glaze Cubes](./sesame-sauce-cubes.md)** + 2 tbsp water. |
| **Toasted Sesame Oil** | 1 tsp | **Off-heat finishing oil only.** |
| **Fresh Basil** (Thai or Sweet) | 6–10 leaves | *Thai Basil* is preferred (adds anethole for TRPV3 support). |
| **Finishers** | 1 tbsp | Toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions. |

---

## Method

1.  **Allicin Activation (00:00):** Mince the 4–6 garlic cloves and let them rest on your cutting board for **10 minutes** before cooking.
2.  **Blanch:** Bring a pot of water to a boil, blanch the green beans for 2–3 minutes until bright green, then drain.
3.  **Sear Mushrooms:** Heat **2 tbsp avocado oil** in a pan over high heat. Add the mushrooms and sear until browned and most of their moisture has evaporated.
4.  **Aromatics (00:30 max):** Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add the rested garlic and grated ginger, cooking for just **30 seconds** to release flavor without destroying the heat-sensitive allicin.
5.  **Toss Veggies:** Add the blanched green beans to the pan and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
6.  **Glaze:** Pour in the teriyaki sauce (or melt in your 2 frozen Soy-Sesame cubes + 2 tbsp water) and cook until it becomes glossy and coats the vegetables.
7.  **Off-Heat Finish:** **Turn off the heat.** Drizzle the toasted sesame oil, toss to combine, and fold in the sliced basil leaves just before serving.
8.  **Garnish:** Top with toasted sesame seeds, green onions, and a splash of lime juice (to enhance iron absorption).
